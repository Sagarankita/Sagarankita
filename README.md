### 🎯 [Beyond Ranking: Department Resource Allocation Optimizer](https://github.com/sagarankita/Beyond-Ranking)
A decision-support framework that finds how a department should split limited money, faculty effort and infrastructure across competing goals. It compares six optimizers (NSGA-II, NSGA-III, MOEA/D, MOPSO, RION and random search) on equal evaluation budgets.
**Skills used:** `multi-objective optimisation` · `evolutionary algorithms` · `DEA benchmarking` · `Random Forest KPI models` · `Monte Carlo robustness analysis` · `statistical testing (Wilcoxon, Holm correction)` · `Python` · `reproducible experiments`

### 🏥 [Urban Health Intelligence System](https://github.com/sagarankita/Urban-Health-Intelligence-System)
A mobile health platform that connects patients, hospitals and city authorities, with outbreak heatmaps, live bed availability, health advisories and AI-based risk assessment.
**Skills used:** `React Native (Expo)` · `Node.js / Express` · `PostgreSQL (migrations + seeding)` · `REST API design` · `Python analytics pipeline` · `outbreak / geospatial analytics`
